GYM INTERVAL CLOCK
==================

A full-screen interval timer for projecting onto a gym wall.


GETTING STARTED
---------------

BEFORE YOU UNZIP - this saves a warning later:

  1. Right-click the downloaded GymClock.zip and choose Properties
  2. Tick "Unblock" at the bottom, then OK
  3. Now unzip it

That tells Windows you know where the file came from, and the app will open
without complaint. If you unzipped first and get "Windows protected your PC",
click "More info" then "Run anyway" - or delete it, unblock the zip, and
unzip again.

THEN:

1. Copy GymClock.exe anywhere you like - your desktop is fine. It needs nothing
   installed and does not write to anywhere except your own user profile.

2. Double-click it. It opens full screen. Press SPACE to start.


THE CONTROLS
------------

  SPACE or click ....... start, pause, resume
  R .................... reset to the beginning
  S .................... change the work and rest times
  1 to 9 ............... instantly apply a saved preset
  RIGHT / LEFT ......... skip forward or back an interval
  M .................... mute the beeps
  F11 .................. leave or re-enter full screen
  F6 ................... move the clock onto the projector
  F3 ................... licence and version information
  Q .................... quit

Most presentation clickers will step the intervals using the arrow keys.

computer. When you ask for a licence renewal, those totals are included in the
email - you will see them in full before you press Send. This is how free teaching
licences are told apart from commercial use.

files, and it never sends anything on its own.
